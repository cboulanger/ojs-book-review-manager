For journals that publish book reviews. The plugin manages the whole path from a work offered for review to the published review:

  • a public list of works offered for review, with metadata looked up by DOI (Crossref) or ISBN;
  • a book review editor role, with a responsible editor for each offer;
  • direct reservation or bidding, and invitations to reviewers (one by one, in a sequential round, or all at once) with one-click links;
  • review agreements with due dates and reminders;
  • review copies tracked from the request to the publisher to their receipt and, if needed, their return;
  • review essays covering several works;
  • a history of every step and email, editable email templates (English, German, Finnish) and a REST API for imports.

Authors attach the reviewed work to their submission, and readers see it on the article page. Data of the Objects for Review plugin can be imported.
